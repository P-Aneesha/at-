1. Information we collect

In this demo, details you enter (such as name, email, mobile number and application data) are stored only in your own browser using localStorage. Nothing is sent to a server.

2. How information is used

Information is used to simulate registration, login, loan applications, dashboards and enquiries so you can evaluate the product experience.

3. Sensitive data

Please do not enter real government ID numbers, bank details or other sensitive personal data. Document uploads in this demo are placeholders and are not transmitted anywhere.

4. Cookies and local storage

We use browser storage to keep your demo session and sample records. You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

5. Third parties

This demo does not connect to banks, credit bureaus, payment gateways or any external financial institution.

Questions about this policy? Reach us through the contact page.
